# Start Here: Your Manual for the New Scam Age

Scams are as old as humans, and so is the advice "don't fall for them." But
something changed in the last few years, and it deserves a real manual:

Scammers now have **perfect writing, believable photos, and your own voice.**

That's it, in one sentence. Artificial intelligence removed the old tells —
the broken grammar, the pixelated pictures, the robotic phone call — that used
to warn us away. What's left is a short list of checks that still work, and
this handbook teaches you exactly those checks.

## The calm promise

This guide is **not** about distrusting everyone. It is a handful of habits
that take seconds and catch almost every AI-powered scam:

- **Pause.** Nothing real needs you to decide in the next two minutes.
- **Verify on a channel you chose.** Hang up and call back on the number you
  already have.
- **Never pay the way a scammer wants.** Gift cards, wire transfers, crypto,
  and "pay to get paid" are the whole ballgame.

That's roughly 80% of the protection. The rest is learning what deepfakes look
like, how voice cloning works, what a fake AI tool site wants from you, and
what to do if you already sent the money.

## Who this is for

Anyone. Specifically anyone who has ever thought *"I would never fall for that"* — because the new scams are built to work on exactly that thought.
You don't need technical knowledge. You need a phone, and eyes, and this
handbook.

## How this fits with the Privacy Comfort Handbook

This guide is the **scam-focused companion** to *The Privacy Comfort Handbook*,
and the two are deliberately set up to not repeat each other:

- **There:** passwords, two-factor login, what never to paste into a chatbot,
  checking whether you're in a data breach, and the anatomy of ordinary
  phishing emails.
- **Here:** scams aimed at *you personally* — messages a machine wrote to
  sound human, photos that never happened, your grandchild's voice asking for
  bail money, fake AI tool websites that collect your logins, deals too good
  to be true, verifying people and payments, and what to do in the painful
  first 24 hours after you fall for one.

When a topic has deep roots over there (like link anatomy in phishing), this
guide will point at it rather than re-teach it.

## How to use it

Read chapters 01, 07 and 08 first if you only have twenty minutes — they
carry the most protection. Then print [the checklist](checklist.md) and put
it near the fridge or on the family noticeboard. When you're ready to talk
about it with the people you love, chapter 09 is the whole plan, scripts
included.

Nothing in here requires software, accounts, or anything beyond what you
already have. This guide works offline and reads like a friend talking —
because that's the only tone a scam manual should have.
